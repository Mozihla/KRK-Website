The problem

Nigeria's lenders are deciding with half the picture.

The system counts transactions. It doesn't know people. Banks see balances, payment platforms see movement, bureaus see borrowing history and often disagree. No one combines these signals.

133Madults in Nigeria
63%of them are banked, about 84 million people
<10%have ever formally borrowed

Sources: UN World Population Prospects 2024; EFInA Access to Finance Survey 2023; World Bank Global Findex 2025.

Thin files, real lives.

Millions earn steadily outside a payslip. Bureau files barely see them, so good borrowers get declined.

Statements nobody can read.

Narrations like PSTKDIRECTDEBITZAP hide what money is really doing. Most tools treat them as noise.

Bureaus that disagree.

A loan on CRC and missing from FirstCentral is a red flag most systems never catch.

Decisions without reasons.

Black-box scores leave risk teams guessing and borrowers in the dark.

The result: lenders say no to people they should approve, and yes to risks they should have seen.

The solution

One layer between Africa's real economy and its lenders.

kranekredit reads every signal the way a seasoned Nigerian credit analyst would, at machine speed, and hands your team one portable answer: the KRK Signal.

How it works

From paperwork to decision in minutes.

Connect. Read. Decide. Your rules and your team stay in control. kranekredit recommends, you decide.

The applicant consents and shares their bank statement, by PDF upload or connected account.

Questions

No. We work alongside the bureaus and add what they can't see, then combine it all into one decision.

You do. kranekredit gives you the clearest picture and a recommendation. Your rules and your team stay in control.

How long does integration take?

Most teams can run a first live check within days.

Which banks do you support?

Statements from major Nigerian banks and digital banks, by PDF or connected account.

Do borrowers know their data is used?

Always. Nothing is read without consent.
