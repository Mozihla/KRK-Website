Why kranefinance

Nigeria moves on wheels it doesn't own, and fuel it can't afford.

The people who keep Nigeria's cities moving earn every day. Most rent the vehicle they drive, buy fuel at the pump each morning, and never get the chance to own anything.

Renting, never owning.

Daily "balance" and work-and-pay deals take the profit from drivers and riders, and the vehicle still belongs to someone else.

Fuel eats the margin.

Since the end of the petrol subsidy, fuel has become the biggest daily cost of moving people and goods.

Credit can't see them.

Banks ask for payslips and collateral. Transport entrepreneurs have neither, so they get declined.

No network to keep EVs moving.

An electric vehicle is only as good as the next charge, the next battery and the next repair. That network barely exists yet.

kranefinance fixes all four at once: the vehicle, the credit, the energy and the upkeep.

FAQs

What drivers and partners ask us first.

Which vehicles do you finance?

Electric cars, motorcycles, tricycles, scooters, cargo vans and e-bicycles. We also still finance trusted fuel sedans such as the Toyota Corolla for drivers not yet on a charging route, and we are open to other vehicle requests.

Do you sell the vehicles?

No. We finance them. You choose from our list of trusted manufacturers and dealers, we buy the vehicle, and you own it on your final payment.

How long can repayments last?

It depends on the vehicle and the amount, up to a maximum of 36 months. Two-wheelers usually run 12 to 18 months.

How fast can I get on the road?

Our target is 24 hours from application to decision, inspection and papers, once your documents are complete.

Do I need a deposit?

For some plans, yes. Many plans start with no deposit. A deposit lowers your weekly payment.

What happens when my battery runs low?

Two- and three-wheelers swap a flat pack for a charged one at any swap station in a few minutes. Cars charge at partner hubs and depots. Worn packs are replaced under your plan.

Who maintains the vehicle?

Scheduled maintenance is part of your plan, done by EV-trained technicians at partner workshops. We remind you before each service is due.

Is the vehicle insured?

Yes. Every vehicle we finance comes with comprehensive insurance, built into the weekly payment.
